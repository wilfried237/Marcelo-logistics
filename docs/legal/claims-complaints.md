# Claims and complaints policy: DRAFT for owner and legal review (not approved)

What already exists in the application:

- customers can report an issue on a shipment from their account; this is recorded in the case history and staff are alerted by email;
- the contact form creates an enquiry with a reference;
- staff see both in the Staff area.

Everything about deadlines, compensation and escalation is an open decision.

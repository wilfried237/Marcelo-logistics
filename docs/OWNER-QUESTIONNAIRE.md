## 5. Quotations ★

Until real prices are approved, the website will **not** calculate prices automatically. Customers will send a request, and your team will reply with a quote.

14. Who on your team receives and answers quote requests? By email, WhatsApp, or both?
15. What response time can you honestly promise, if any? (For example, "within 2 working days".)
16. What information do you need from a customer to prepare a quote, for each service? (For example: number and size of barrels; vehicle make, model and year; contents list.)
17. How long is a quote valid?
18. Would you like customers to be able to upload photos or documents with a request? If yes, which ones?

## 6. Pricing (later, for automatic prices)

21. Are customs duties and taxes in Cameroon included, optional, or always paid by the recipient?
